Purpose and authorised use

COGS Docket Collector is an internal operational tool provided by Junk Specialist Sydney Pty Ltd. Use is limited to authorised business activities and administrators. There is no public subscription or self-service sign-up.

Business records and review

Submit only information you are authorised to provide for business purposes. Automated extraction and matching can be incomplete or incorrect. Authorised staff must review docket details, sender mappings, duplicate flags, rates and allocations before relying on financial reports.

Account access and availability

Keep credentials and account access secure. The workflow depends on external services and may be interrupted by service changes, authentication expiry or connection failures. The operator may update or pause it for maintenance or to correct issues.

Privacy and support

Information handling is described in our privacy policy. For support or questions about use, contact sydneyjunkcogs@gmail.com.
