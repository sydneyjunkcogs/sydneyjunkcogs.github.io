COGS Docket Collector

An internal tool for Junk Specialist Sydney Pty Ltd that collects disposal and recycling dockets and helps the operations team track daily costs.

How it works

  1. Collects docket photos from the designated business WhatsApp group.
  2. Saves photos to a selected Google Drive folder.
  3. Uses a Google Sheets staff directory to match WhatsApp senders with team member names.
  4. Feeds a separate Google Workspace Studio workflow that extracts docket details for review and reporting.

Google account access

An authorised administrator connects the business account and selects the Drive folder and staff directory. The collector requests access to files it creates or that are explicitly authorised for use with the app, using the Google Drive drive.file permission.

This is the public information site. Operational photos, staff records and the collector dashboard are not published here. There is no public registration or upload service.

Operator and support

Operated by Junk Specialist Sydney Pty Ltd for its Sydney franchise operations. This tool is not a Google or WhatsApp product.
