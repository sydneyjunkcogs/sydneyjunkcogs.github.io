COGS Docket Collector

Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

This policy describes the COGS Docket Collector operated by Junk Specialist Sydney Pty Ltd for internal Sydney business operations.

Information processed

The collector processes docket images, message identifiers and timestamps from the designated WhatsApp group. Its staff directory includes WhatsApp display names, phone numbers where available, WhatsApp identifiers, team member names and active or mapping status. Dockets may contain facility names, vehicle registrations, dates, weights, materials and charges.

Google data and permissions

The collector uses Google OAuth with the drive.file scope to access files it creates or that an administrator explicitly authorises. It uploads docket images and associated metadata to a selected Drive folder, and reads and updates the selected Google Sheets staff directory. It stores Google authorisation tokens to continue this authorised work without repeated sign-in.

Purpose and access

Information is used to identify docket senders, associate receipts with business records, maintain the staff directory, prevent repeat processing and support cost reporting. Authorised operations staff review the resulting records. A connected Google Workspace Studio workflow uses Gemini to extract information from docket images and writes it to the business spreadsheet; extracted results require review.

Storage and service providers

Google Drive and Sheets store the selected business files. The collector runs on a DigitalOcean server and stores its WhatsApp session, authorisation tokens, local photo copies, processing queue and directory mappings. WhatsApp, Google, DigitalOcean and GitHub provide relevant messaging, processing, storage or website hosting services under their respective terms. This public website does not display operational records.

Sharing and safeguards

The operator does not sell this information or use it for advertising. Access is limited to the operator, authorised staff and providers involved in delivering the workflow, or disclosures required by law. Account permissions and server access controls protect operational data and credentials. The collector does not use Google user data to train general-purpose AI models.

Retention and deletion

Records are retained for operational and recordkeeping needs. The collector does not currently apply a fixed automatic deletion schedule to all stored copies. Authorised administrators can arrange removal of directory entries, local files and Google records, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements. Removing a member from the WhatsApp group does not automatically erase historical records.

Your choices and contact

Contact sydneyjunkcogs@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion, or ask a privacy question. An account administrator can revoke the app's access through their Google Account connections settings. Revocation prevents further authorised access but does not by itself delete existing business records.

This website

These pages contain no analytics scripts, tracking cookies or data-entry forms added by the operator. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical request information under its own privacy statement. Changes to this policy will be published on this page.
